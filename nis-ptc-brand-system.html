Official palette in the booking flow

Six supplied brand colors, with green reserved for success. White surfaces keep the mobile booking flow light. Dark red appears in the logo and the primary action. Content, surfaces, and secondary actions stay neutral. Error and cutoff states use clear words and icons.

Weights: 400 body and confirmation values, 500 confirmation heading, 600 section headings, labels, and actions. Mobile inputs remain at least 16px to avoid browser zoom.
